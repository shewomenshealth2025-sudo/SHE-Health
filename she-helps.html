Why it matters

More than a symptom checker.

SHE Helps is a navigation layer. It turns scattered symptoms, questions and uncertainty into clear educational routes — helping users understand what may be relevant, what to read next, what products may support them, and where to seek help.

Built around patterns

Women can understand recurring symptoms, timing, triggers and context more clearly.

Connected to support

Education links into SHE Facts, SHE Finds and location-based service signposting.

Safe by design

SHE Helps is educational and supportive. It does not diagnose, treat or replace medical advice.
