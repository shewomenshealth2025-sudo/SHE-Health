SHE Health exists to make women’s health easier to navigate.

We are building the navigation layer that connects women to education, support, products, services and institutions.

Mission

To make women’s health clearer, more accessible and more connected.

Approach

Education, navigation, community, partnerships and events working together as infrastructure.

Values

Clarity, safety, credibility, accessibility, partnership and impact.

Founder note

SHE began from the need to make women’s health more understandable, accessible and connected.
