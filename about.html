About SHE Health

Women’s health, made easier to navigate.

SHE connects information, support and people across women’s health. We are building a platform and creating spaces for the conversations that move care forward.

Explore the platformExplore the Forum

Why SHE exists

Too many pieces. Too little connection.

Finding trustworthy information, understanding care options and knowing where to turn can take far too much work. SHE began with a simple idea: make women’s health clearer and easier to navigate.

Our work brings together a growing digital platform, the SHE Women’s Health Forum and its published outputs. Each helps connect people, knowledge and services around women’s health.
