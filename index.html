Women are expected to self-navigate Google, social media, waitlists, forums, and fragmented services.

The platform

One umbrella. Six connected layers.

SHE is not just content, not just a tracker, and not just a community. It is a connected women’s health navigation system.

◇

SHE Helps

Guided symptom-to-action support that helps women understand possible routes, next steps, and local services.

✓

SHE Facts

Evidence-based education across periods, PCOS, endometriosis, contraception, fertility, menopause, and more.

★

SHE Finds

Structured product guidance for period care and women-centric health products, without the overwhelm.

◌

SHE Asks

A safer community layer for questions, lived experience, and real conversations around women’s health.

◉

SHE Tells

Curated insights, interviews, and masterclasses with professionals across healthcare, science, sport, and innovation.

▤

SHE News

Policy, research, femtech, and public health updates translated into clear, useful takeaways.
