What is SHE?

The app and convener for women’s health navigation.

SHE brings together the fragmented pieces of women’s health into one connected place — helping women understand what they’re experiencing, explore practical next steps, and find support without having to search across endless silos.

📱

An app

A clear, user-friendly demo of how women could navigate symptoms, education, products and support.

🧭

A navigation layer

Designed to turn scattered information into clearer pathways and more confident next steps.

🤝

A convener

Bringing healthcare, policy, education, workplaces, innovation and lived experience into the same conversation.

✨

One connected place

Because women should not have to piece together their health journey alone.
