What is SHE?

Women’s Health, Made Easy.

SHE is a women’s health navigation platform designed to help women better understand their health, explore their options, access trusted information and identify the right services, products and support — through one app and through meaningful real-world connection.

Women’s health remains highly fragmented, with information, care pathways, services and support often spread across multiple systems and sources. SHE brings these elements together into a clearer, more accessible and more connected experience.

📱

The app

Trusted information, health navigation, products, services and support brought together in one accessible place.

🧭

Personal navigation

Helping women understand what may be relevant to them, explore their options and identify practical next steps.

🤝

In person

Creating opportunities for education, community and direct connection with women’s health experts, services and organisations.

✨

One connected experience

Bringing together the parts of women’s health that women currently have to navigate separately.
