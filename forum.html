SHE Women’s Health Forum · Stormont 2026

The conversation doesn’t end in the room.

On 9 September 2026, SHE Health brought policy, healthcare, research, advocacy and lived experience together in the Long Gallery at Parliament Buildings, Stormont.

Read the reportView the gallery

What happened

A room built for a wider conversation.

Two panels examined the case for a Women’s Health Strategy for Northern Ireland and the everyday realities of care and support. Policymakers, clinicians, researchers, advocates, community organisations and women with lived experience joined the discussion.

Fragmented information and pathways, prevention, stigma, inequality and the need to listen to women were recurring themes. SHE’s published paper carries the discussion beyond the event.

Published September 2026 · Outcomes & recommendations

SHE Women’s Health Forum 2026: Outcomes & Recommendations Report

This 63-page report records the panel discussions, the themes raised in the room and 15 recommendations for a more coordinated approach to women’s health in Northern Ireland. It is intended to inform continued policy and service discussions.

Read the reportDownload PDF

From the published paper

Five priorities for what comes next.

01

A long-term strategy

Build on the existing Women’s Health Action Plan with clear priorities, timelines and accountability.

02

Women’s voices

Make lived experience part of policy design, implementation and evaluation.

03

Clearer care pathways

Review gaps in referral routes, diagnosis, treatment and follow-up.

04

Prevention and education

Improve accessible information and early intervention across the life course.

05

Measure progress

Track access, outcomes, inequalities and women’s experiences of care.
