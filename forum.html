SHE Women’s Health Forum 2026

Bringing together the people shaping the future of women’s health in Northern Ireland.

Wednesday 9 September 2026 · 9:30am–12:30pm · Long Gallery, Parliament Buildings, Stormont. A focused cross-sector forum bringing together healthcare, policy, research, advocacy, education, industry and lived experience.

About the Forum.

Women’s health in Northern Ireland is at an important point. Information, services and support remain fragmented, while Northern Ireland continues without a dedicated Women’s Health Strategy.

The SHE Women’s Health Forum brings together people working across the system to examine where the gaps remain, what can be learned from elsewhere and what meaningful progress could look like for women in Northern Ireland.

The focus is not simply awareness, but constructive conversation that can strengthen connections, clarify priorities and support practical action.

Two conversations. One shared goal.

To make women’s health more connected, accessible and easier to navigate.

  • Building a Women’s Health Strategy for Northern Ireland
  • Women’s Health in Practice
  • Cross-sector collaboration
  • Healthcare, policy and lived experience
