Why the forum exists

Women’s health is now a strategic priority across the UK and Ireland, but Northern Ireland is still at an earlier stage of building a dedicated, joined-up approach.

The SHE Women’s Health Forum brings together policy, public health, clinical practice, education, workplace wellbeing, innovation and lived experience — conversations that rarely happen in the same room.

Policy changes the rules. Infrastructure changes outcomes.
