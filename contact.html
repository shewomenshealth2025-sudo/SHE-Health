Why SHE Health exists.

Women’s health is not short of information. It is short of clear pathways. Too often, women are left moving between Google searches, social media, long waits, unclear advice, product confusion and fragmented support.

SHE Health exists to close that gap. It brings together education, navigation, practical support, lived experience and institutional action so women can move from uncertainty to clearer next steps.

The app is the infrastructure layer: helping users understand symptoms, access SHE Facts, compare support through SHE Finds, and move towards services and practical action through SHE Helps.

Information is available. Navigation is not.

The SHE Forum extends this mission at system level; convening government, healthcare, public health, universities, workplaces, innovation and community voices to advance women’s health in Northern Ireland and beyond.

  • Education that women can understand and use
  • Support that connects symptoms to action
  • Partnerships that improve access and health literacy
  • Forum conversations that shape policy and infrastructure
